01 / ABOUT

Yibin Dong

I am Yibin Dong (董怡斌), a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Frontier and Interdisciplinary Science, Shandong University.

My research focuses on atomistic simulations of defects and interfaces, their motion and interactions, and the mechanisms governing plastic deformation and fracture. I work with molecular dynamics and first-principles methods to study materials under irradiation and mechanical loading.
